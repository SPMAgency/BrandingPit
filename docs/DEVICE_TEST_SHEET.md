# Device-test sheet — what "right" looks like on the phone

For Doctor Def's phone tests. One row per check: what to do, what right looks
like. Owners decide; this sheet only says what to look for. Headless tests
never replace these.

| Check | How | Right looks like | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frame rate | Play 3 full Raids, profiler on | No stall over 0.25 s; steady 60 fps on a mid-range phone, 30 on a low-end one | Engineering |
| Sprite memory | 16 different fighters on screen (Roadmap M1) | Under the 71 MB budget (Animation Reboot plan §3); no browser reload | Animations |
| Raid tension | Play 5 Raids | 15 s feels tense, not rushed; you can brand 3 targets and catch a runner if you're quick | Actions/Functions |
| Tap targets | Tap every button with a thumb | Nothing missed or hit by accident; nothing under 44 px (UI audit) | Engineering |
| Short screen | A 360 x 640 phone, or the smallest he has | Whole board, bench and controls visible; fighters at least about 39 pt tall | Arena, Engineering |
| Unit card | Press and hold a fighter; hover with a mouse on desktop | Card opens, reads clearly, updates during the fight; a quick tap still brands | Engineering |
| Hit feel | Watch crits, knockouts and a boss ability | Hit-stop and flash noticeable but never more than twice a second; off with reduced motion on | Arena |
| Sound | Every event, first tap starts audio | Each event audible, no pile-ups in a big fight | Sound/Music |

Turn every switch back to its default after testing unless its owner says otherwise.
